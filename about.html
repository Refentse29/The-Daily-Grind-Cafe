Our Story

Founded in 2026, The Daily Grind Cafe started with a simple vision: to bring ethically sourced, freshly roasted artisanal coffee to our local community.

We work directly with sustainable farmers to ensure every bean is grown responsibly and roasted to perfection.

Our Mission

To deliver an uncompromised coffee experience while supporting sustainable farming communities across Africa.
